£220k Second Home — Total Cashflow Assessment

Yorkshire coast · September 2026 · rates indicative, confirm with a broker

Verdict: ~£73k cash in, then ~£1,350–1,500/month (~£16–18k/yr) to hold for maybe 20–30 nights' use — roughly £600–700 per night used. It only stacks up financially if it doubles as a rental between visits, or you treat the carry as the price of a 10-year lifestyle asset.

What you'd be liable for

  • Stamp duty £12,900 — non-recoverable unless you later sell your main home and claim a refund within 3 years (GOV.UK rates)
  • Double council tax — East Riding is one of 211+ councils with the 100% premium (2026 list)
  • No CGT-free sale — gains on a second home are liable for CGT at 18/24% (GOV.UK)
  • Income tax if ever let — triggers the FHL / rental regime
  • Affordability — the £915/mo commitment counts against income alongside your existing mortgage

Ways it makes more sense

  • Rental offset — letting it between your visits typically covers 50–70% of the carry
  • Same money, rented base — the £73k as a buy-to-let deposit, tenant subsidises, you book stays in it
  • Lifestyle asset — accept ~£17k/yr as the cost of having it, decide on a 10-year horizon
